What this site stores

This site stores your cookie preference in localStorage on your device. It remembers whether you selected Accept or Decline so the preference panel does not reappear on every page.

No advertising trackers

This version does not include advertising pixels or analytics trackers. Choosing Accept does not enable any additional tracking.

Hosting

The hosting platform may use technical mechanisms necessary to provide access and protect the service. These are controlled by the hosting platform.

Changing your choice

Use Cookie settings in the footer to reopen the preference panel. You can also clear this website’s saved data through your browser settings.

Future changes

If optional analytics or advertising tools are added, the policy and consent controls must be updated before those tools are enabled.
